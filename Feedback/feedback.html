Climate Action Programmes

Explore a range of sustainability initiatives designed to help students make a real environmental impact. Whether you're interested in conservation, renewable energy, community outreach, or reducing waste, there's a programme for everyone. Join a project, learn new skills, and become part of a growing community committed to a greener future.

Campus Tree Planting

Help make our campus greener by planting native trees that absorb carbon, improve air quality, and support local biodiversity. Join fellow students in creating a healthier environment for future generations.

River Clean-Up Crew

Take part in restoring our local waterways by removing litter, monitoring water quality, and protecting aquatic ecosystems alongside the Environmental Society.

Zero-Waste Challenge

Sustainable Commute Week

Choose walking, cycling, carpooling, or public transport instead of driving. Track your carbon savings and inspire others to adopt greener travel habits.
