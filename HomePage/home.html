Small Choices. Real Climate Action.

ClimACT is a student-led hub for SDG 13, turning the science of climate change into everyday actions students and local communities can actually take.

Climate change is no longer a distant threat described in textbooks.
It is the wildfire smoke drifting over campus, the flooded commute, the rising cost of a weekly food shop.
SDG - 13, Climate Action, calls on everyone, not just governments, to act with urgency. ClimACT exists to make that call practical: clear explanations, honest numbers, and concrete steps that students, staff, and neighbours can put into practice this week, not "someday."

Our Mission

Three ideas ClimACT is built around.

Why Climate Action Can't Wait

Every extra tenth of a degree of global warming increases the risks facing people, ecosystems, and future generations. Rising temperatures contribute to more frequent and intense heatwaves, floods, droughts, wildfires, and rising sea levels, affecting communities around the world. Acting now is far more effective and less costly than dealing with the long-term impacts of climate change later. By reducing emissions, protecting natural ecosystems, and adopting sustainable practices today, we can help create a healthier, safer, and more resilient future for everyone.

  • Learn where your own emissions actually come from
  • Vote and campaign with climate policy in mind
  • Push your course, halls, or workplace to publish a carbon plan

Powering Tomorrow, Today

Renewable energy is no longer just a promise for the futur. It is already one of the most affordable and sustainable sources of electricity in many parts of the world. By replacing fossil fuels with clean energy from the sun, wind, water, and other renewable sources, we can reduce greenhouse gas emissions, improve air quality, and create a more sustainable future. Supporting the transition to renewable energy is one of the most effective ways individuals, communities, businesses, and governments can help tackle climate change while building a cleaner and more resilient world.

  • Switch household or hall energy tariffs to a renewable supplier
  • Report unused lighting and heating in shared buildings
  • Support local renewable planning applications instead of opposing them

Small Habits, Real Change

Everyone has the power to make a positive impact through simple everyday choices. Small actions such as walking, cycling, using public transport, reducing energy consumption, recycling, and avoiding single-use plastics can significantly lower carbon emissions over time. While one person's efforts may seem small, when adopted by millions of people, these habits create meaningful environmental change. Together, everyday actions contribute to a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable future for our planet.

  • Walk or cycle journeys under 3 km instead of driving
  • Batch errands into one trip rather than several
  • Try one plant-forward meal swap a week
